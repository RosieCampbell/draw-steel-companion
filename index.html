How every roll works — read this once
Attack or test: roll 2d10 + my stat (usually +2). Total 11 or less → the FIRST number listed · 12–16 → the SECOND · 17+ → the THIRD. So “5/8/15” means weak / solid / great — I can’t miss, only hit softer or harder.
My stat does double duty on attacks. The book writes damage as “3 + Might” — so my stat is added to the 2d10 roll (to find the tier) and to the damage. This sheet has already added my +2 to every damage number — so I add +2 to the dice, read the tier, and the damage shown is final.
Dice showing a natural 19–20 on a main-action attack: a critical hit — I immediately take another action (the result is always tier 3 regardless of modifiers). On a test: success with a reward, at any difficulty.
Common bonuses: an edge is +2, a bane is −2. Any dotted word on this sheet is tappable for a reminder — try those two.
Distances: M1 = melee, target adjacent · R15 = ranged, within 15 squares (a square ≈ 5 ft). Each of my turns = 1 main action + 1 maneuver + 1 move, any order, movement splittable.
Badges: Mine = from my class, ancestry & kits · Everyone = universal rules any hero can use (shown with my numbers). Color code: red = it costs me something · green = it comes to me free.

③ That’s a full, solid turn. Tap ▶ My turn below when my turn starts; say “done” when it ends.

Conditions — when one lands on me (or them)
Bleeding — whenever they use a main action, a triggered action, or roll with Might or Agility, they lose 1d6 + level Stamina afterward; can't be prevented. (While dying, I'm also bleeding.)
Dazed — one thing per turn: a main action, a maneuver, or a move. No triggered actions (including free triggered actions) or free maneuvers.
Frightened — bane attacking the source of fear; the source gets an edge attacking them; can't willingly move toward it.
Grabbed — speed 0; bane on anything that doesn't target the grabber; can't be force-moved except by them. Escape Grab (maneuver) or a teleport ends it.
Prone — their strikes take a bane; melee against them gets an edge; crawling costs double movement. Stand Up = a maneuver.
Restrained — speed 0, can't stand up, can't be force-moved; bane on ability rolls and Might/Agility tests; abilities against them get an edge. Teleporting ends it.
Slowed — speed becomes 2 (unless already lower), and no shifting.
Taunted — double bane on any ability that doesn't target the taunter, while they have line of effect to them. (This is what my Protective Attack inflicts.)
Weakened — bane on all their power rolls. (Mind Game inflicts this.)
Two mercies: the same condition from two sources doesn't stack — nobody is weakened “twice” · when combat ends, conditions on me end if I want them to (only winded, unconscious, and dying persist).
