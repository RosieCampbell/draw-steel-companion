02

Your core loop — Focus & Mark

The Tactician's whole identity. Focus is your fuel; Mark turns the party's damage into a toolbox. This is the rhythm of almost every fight.

Earn Focus

Gain 2 Focus at the start of each of your turns. +1 the first time each round you or an ally damage your Marked target, and +1 the first time an ally within 10 squares uses a heroic ability. (At the start of combat, you also gain Focus equal to your Victories.)

Mark a foe

Spend a maneuver to Mark the scariest enemy. Now you and your allies get an edge on every roll against it, for the whole fight.

Cash in on hits

Every time you or an ally deals rolled damage to it — even on their turns — you can spend 1 Focus for one of: +4 damage, letting the attacker spend one of their own Recoveries without an action, a free 2-square shift, or — only when the melee hit was your own — a taunt. One benefit per hit.

Command the field

Pour Focus into tempo: Strike Now! lets an ally act again, Squad! Forward! moves the team, Overwatch punishes movement, and Mind Game re-Marks while it wounds.

You lose unspent Focus at the end of combat — so spend freely. And thanks to Useful Emotion, you start every fight with a free surge already in the bank.

Decoder ring — six terms everything below uses

Edge / bane: +2 / −2 on a roll. Two of the same kind don’t add up — they push the result a whole tier better or worse. · EoT: lasts “until the End of the target’s next Turn.” · Save ends: the effect sticks until the sufferer rolls a d10 at the end of their turn and gets 6+. · Signature ability: a hero’s basic class attack, always free — vs a heroic ability, which requires spending class resource (your Focus). · Free strike: the plain sword-swing or bow-shot anyone can always make. · Shift: a careful step that never provokes free hits from enemies.

E

Two mechanics to know cold

How surges work

A surge is a stored "+2 damage" chit. When you deal ability damage, spend up to 3 on one target (max +6). Or spend 2 surges to raise a potency by 1 (surges can only ever add +1 potency). They disappear at the end of combat.

How potency works

An effect like "R<1" means the target resists if its Reason is 1 or higher. Your potencies are weak 0 / average 1 / strong 2, so low-Reason foes get slowed by Overwatch while sharp ones shrug it off. Spend 2 surges to bump it.

03

Edges & Banes

Situational advantages are edges; disadvantages are banes. One of either is worth ±2. Stack two and you don't add a number at all — you shift the outcome a whole tier. They cap at two each, and they cancel one-for-one. Stack them up and watch the verdict.

Banes

Why the cap? Every advantage has diminishing returns — once you're prone and poisoned, a net on top can't really make it worse. Capping at two also keeps the table fast.

No edges or banes apply. Roll 2d10 + your characteristic and read the tier straight off.

04

Combat

Fights are short and tactical — most last three rounds or fewer because everyone deals damage on every turn. Combat runs on a square grid, and the two sides alternate picking who acts.

Your turn = three things

Take them in any order, and break up your movement around them however you like.

⚔

Main action

Your big play — a class ability, a charge, a free strike, defend, or heal.

↻

Maneuver

A lighter move — grab, knockback, catch your breath, drink a potion, make a test.

→

Move action

Advance up to your speed, or disengage to shift safely past foes.

Trade down: you can convert your main action into another maneuver or move action — so a turn can be two maneuvers + a move, or two moves + a maneuver. Plus free maneuvers (open a door, draw a weapon) and one triggered action per round when its trigger fires.

Surprise & first move

Anyone not ready is surprised until the end of round one (no triggered actions; attacks against them get an edge). To see who acts first, a player rolls a d10 — on a 6+, the heroes choose which side begins.

Opportunity attacks

If an adjacent enemy walks away without shifting, you get a free melee strike. Shifting (a careful step) avoids this — but you can't shift through difficult or damaging terrain.

Hero tokens

A shared pool starting equal to your party size. Spend them to reroll a test, succeed on a save, gain surges, or regain Stamina. Earn more by taking big, selfless risks.

Saving throws

Some effects end "(save ends)." At the end of your turn, roll a d10 — on a 6 or higher, the effect ends. Pure luck; spend a hero token to auto-succeed.

Forced movement

Push away, pull toward, slide anywhere — your Knockback shoves 1/2/3. A target's stability cancels that many squares. Slam someone into a wall or another creature for extra damage; forced movement ignores difficult terrain and never provokes opportunity attacks.

Line of effect

To target anything, an unblocked line must run from your space to theirs. A solid wall breaks it entirely; cover (half-blocked) gives a bane, concealment (fog/dark) gives a bane. Your abilities reach far — Patient Shot at 15, Mark and your commands at 10 — so line of effect, not range, is usually your real limit.

How many times you can catch your breath and keep fighting. Spend one to regain Stamina equal to your recovery value. Refreshed fully after a respite.

Heroic Resource

Your class's fuel — the fury's ferocity, the tactician's focus, the shadow's insight. Earned during combat and spent on your most potent abilities. It encourages pressing on, not resting.

Victories → Experience

Win a fight or beat a big challenge, earn a Victory. Victories boost you mid-adventure, then convert to permanent XP when you finish a respite. (No farming bags of rats — challenges must be worthy.)

Respite

24 uninterrupted hours of rest. Restores all Stamina and Recoveries, converts Victories to XP, and lets you push one downtime project. Ordinary sleep doesn't count.
